The process

The college admissions process should be exciting: a moment of introspection with an eye toward a future brimming with possibility. Ask the average teenager or parent, and the first word that comes to mind is stressful. It doesn't have to be. I've spent the past 20 years helping hundreds of families navigate this transition with ease, empowering applicants to recognize their strengths, find their unique voice, and embrace happiness as a fundamental criteria in plotting out the next four years of their lives.

I consciously limit the number of families I work with, so you can always call, email, or text as questions arise... big or little.

9th & 10th grade

We meet 1–3 times per year for broader strategic planning: academic courses, extracurricular opportunities, summer programs, passion projects, research, and internships. Big picture, we shape your academic profile and plan out meaningful experiences, setting you up to tell the story of how your work has impacted your life, your community, and your future goals.

The story starts early

Everything you do becomes material for the applications to come.

11th grade

We start early and build momentum: one or two meetings total in the fall, then one or two per month from January through June.

1st semester

Lay the foundations

  • Plot a personalized 12th grade course schedule
  • Develop a testing plan that plays to your strengths
  • Create an initial resume and strategize summer plans
  • Map out college visits
  • Apply to summer programs, where they fit
2nd semester

Figure out who you are

  • Research colleges and craft an initial list
  • Structured self-inquiry toward personal essay ideas
  • Major-driven research into academic programs
  • Build relationships with teachers and counselors
  • Finalize enriching summer plans and confirm testing

12th grade

This is it. Starting in July, as applications go live, we meet two to four times per month and I manage your entire process: tracking deadlines and to-dos, and providing support, mentorship, and encouragement for the whole family, every step of the way.
