What is Launch?

Launch is a small group of independent educational consultants (of which I am a founding member) who banded together to crowdsource the services our families ask for again and again. Rather than sending you off to shop for a tutor, a research program, or a career coach on your own, we vetted the best providers, negotiated access, and coordinate their work with your admissions plan, so every session serves the bigger picture.
