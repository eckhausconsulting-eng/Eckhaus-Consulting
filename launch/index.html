What is Launch?

Launch is a small group of independent educational consultants (of which I am a founding member) who banded together to crowdsource the services our families ask for again and again. Rather than sending you off to shop for a tutor, a research program, or a career coach on your own, we vetted the best providers, negotiated access, and coordinate their work with your admissions plan, so every session serves the bigger picture.

How it works

1
We talk it through

Tell me what your student needs. Together we pick the right offering and level of support.

2
You're matched

A coordinator pairs your student with a hand-selected mentor or tutor and builds a personalized plan.

3
Work begins in 48 hours

Your Launch coordinator reaches out within two days of enrollment to schedule the first session.

4
I stay in the loop

Progress is coordinated alongside your admissions plan.
