The pathways

Publication is never guaranteed by any rigorous program; each path includes structured support for submission to selective, college-level journals.
Pathway 1

Mentored Research & Publication

A rigorous, traditional research experience. Students work 1:1 with a PhD fellow or post-doc to build discipline-specific depth and produce a polished, publishable paper, with dedicated writing-coach and publication support.

  • Ideal for rigorous, publication-focused research
  • 15 mentor sessions + writing & publication support
  • 3–4 months research · 1–4 months publication
  • Outcome: publication in a college-level journal

Pathway 2

Research & Passion Projects

A flexible, project-based path for students exploring their interests. Begin with three mentors to shape the idea, then build the project you want... a research paper, a website, an app, or a video essay.

  • Ideal for exploring interests or a creative project
  • 16 one-on-one sessions (Launchpad · Core · Showcasing)
  • The student chooses the output
  • Outcome: a showcase-ready project of your choosing
View the brochure (PDF) →
Pathway 3

Research Scholar Program

A 10-week, self-paced sprint connecting students with Ivy League and Oxbridge mentors to produce publishable research, with strong live 1:1 writing support in a structured format.

  • Ideal for a focused, publication-oriented sprint
  • 8 mentor sessions + 8–12 writing-coach sessions
  • 90% publication success rate in reputable journals
  • Outcome: a publishable research paper

How it works

Apply & interview

Complete a short application; shortlisted students are scheduled for an interview.

Mentor match

Selected students are matched with a mentor in their area of research interest.

Find the question

Early sessions align on a topic and sharpen it into a focused, answerable research question.

Research & write

Students build the design, run the analysis, and draft the paper with mentor and writing-coach support.

Finalize & submit

Final edits, then structured support to submit to selective high-school or college-level journals.
