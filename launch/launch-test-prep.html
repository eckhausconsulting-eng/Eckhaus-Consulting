SAT Prep for Diverse Thinkers

Some students are strong in class and sharp in conversation, but the SAT score just doesn't show it. Usually the barrier isn't ability, it's that standard prep is built for one kind of brain. This is specialized, one-on-one SAT prep for students who process the test differently, built for the digital SAT.

◑
ADHD, anxiety & executive function

For students whose timing, focus, or test anxiety drags the score below what they know.

◐
Learning & processing differences

Dyslexia, dyscalculia, dysgraphia, or visual-processing differences like Irlen Syndrome.

◱
Accommodations, handled

Extended time, a 504 or IEP, or pending accommodations, worked into the plan from day one. No diagnosis required to start.

◆
Prep that didn't stick

Strong GPA but a stuck score, or a course or tutor that already came and went without moving the score.

Why it's different

It starts with how your student's brain works, not a generic script

The SAT isn't only testing what a student knows, it tests how fast they can retrieve, prioritize, and apply it under pressure. Standard programs teach the same content and strategy to everyone. This one begins with a diagnostic done a different way, reasoning through every answer to reveal exactly where points are lost, then builds a personalized plan around it: targeted practice instead of endless full tests, two sessions a week for rhythm, a midpoint progress check, and dedicated pacing and anxiety work for test day.
