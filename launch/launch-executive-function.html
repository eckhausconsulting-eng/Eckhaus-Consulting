What is executive function coaching?

Executive functions are the brain processes behind planning, organization, decision-making, and goal setting. In school they're what let a student manage coursework, deadlines, and growing independence. Bright, capable students often struggle here because these skills develop unevenly through the teenage years. Through a research-based system, coaching makes them explicit and builds strategies that stick.
