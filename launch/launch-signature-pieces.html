Why do a Signature Piece?

Your impact sets you apart

Colleges give applicants both an Academic Score and a Personal Score, and a strong Personal Score improves admissions chances by up to 10x. Yet only 1 in 5 applicants earns a strong one. Signature Pieces are how a student moves from participation to accomplishment, producing concrete proof that brings essays and interviews to life: I accomplished X, as measured by Y, by doing Z.

◆
Colleges care about impact

Completing real work with measurable outcomes shows initiative, follow-through, and the ability to create impact.

❝
Your story needs evidence

Strong applications are built on action, and Signature Pieces are the evidence.

◎
Fit to major matters

Exploring an interest in a real-world setting demonstrates fit, especially for competitive majors.

★
Mentorship raises the bar

Guidance from experienced mentors turns strong ideas into standout outcomes.

The five skills

What a Signature Piece teaches

Every Signature Piece develops the skills that aren't taught in a high-school classroom: Agency: intentionally setting goals and taking action; Critical Thinking: analyzing and synthesizing ideas; Problem Solving: developing and executing solutions; Communication: expressing ideas and collaborating; and Executive Functioning: planning, prioritizing, and managing time.

Original research, worthy of publication

Signature Projects

A one-of-a-kind project, built in 90 days

For students whose story is best told through creation rather than a research paper. With expert guidance, students design and execute a high-impact, deeply personal project, a website, podcast, e-book, business, or advocacy campaign, that showcases initiative, curiosity, and follow-through. Colleges admit students, not transcripts, and a signature project is the kind of story an admissions reader remembers.

Example

The Music & Healing Podcast

Brandon produced five episodes exploring music, wellness, and community, conducting guest interviews, managing production, and building a portfolio that showcased his passion and initiative.

Example

The Cell Tale Series

Aryan authored and self-published a collection of children's books explaining cancer biology in age-appropriate language.

Example

Celiac College Cookbook

Morgan contacted 60+ universities and built a cookbook recognized by the National Celiac Foundation, turning a personal need into a public resource.

Internships

Work a company will actually use

Students complete a project defined by a company, with an outcome the company will actually use, learning from a mentor and gaining hands-on experience rarely available before college. Two formats fit different levels of ambition and time.

Start-up Internship

Inside an ambitious start-up

Interns work directly with founding teams whose CEOs come from institutions like Harvard Business School and firms like McKinsey, Google, and Microsoft.

  • 8 weeks · 5–10 hrs / week
  • 8 sessions with the host company
  • Completed professional project
  • Letter of completion (recommendation possible)
Work Lab Internship

Design your own project

A short-term, project-based experience with start-ups and nonprofits where students design their own work with 1:1 mentor guidance.

  • 6 weeks over the summer · ages 15+
  • 10 hrs training + 40 hrs project work
  • 5 weekly check-ins with a host contact
  • Final project presentation + certificate
Start a Business

Build something from concept to customer

With the support of an experienced entrepreneur mentor, students refine an idea, test it with real users, and bring a product to market. One student became an urban beekeeper of 25,000+ bees, selling "Polluted Honey" with labels that reveal the air-pollution level at harvest.
