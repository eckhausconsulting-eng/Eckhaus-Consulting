How it works

Welcome email & onboarding forms

After signing up, families receive a welcome email and short onboarding forms (best completed within 48 hours) that help match your student with the right tutor.

Onboarding call

Your Tutoring Coordinator, your point of contact throughout, walks you through the tutor match and a personalized schedule built around your preferred times.

Share the coursework

Students share the syllabus, quizzes, and assignments so instruction is tailored to their exact class and any upcoming AP exams.

Introductory session

Students meet their tutor, get an overview of the plan, and begin with the areas that need the most support.

Consistent sessions

Students meet one to three times per week, with focused practice between sessions and progress reports shared along the way.

Understanding that lasts

Students build the understanding and study habits that carry through the course and the exam.
