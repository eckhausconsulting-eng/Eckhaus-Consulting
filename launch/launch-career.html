Why career coaching

The first job search is its own admissions process, and almost nobody prepares students for it. Coaching runs from the first year of college through landing a post-grad position, and students can join at any point in college or in their first working years. Coaches bring hiring, recruiting, and career-development experience from universities and top employers.
